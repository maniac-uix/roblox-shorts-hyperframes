# Research brief — roblox-facts-older-than-you-think

Subject: surprising verified facts about Roblox itself (age, name, scale, creator economy).
Format: ROBLOX FACTS. Target 30–45 s.

## Verified claims (all used in the script)

1. Roblox was created in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel; early in
   development it was known as DynaBlocks.
   Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Roblox&oldid=1375277477
   ("Roblox was created in 2004 by co-founders David Baszucki and Erik
   Cassel… Early in its development, Roblox was known as DynaBlocks.")
2. The name "Roblox" combines "robots" and "blocks".
   Source: https://www.vpn4games.com/en/blog/detail/roblox-game-time-line
   ("a name that combines 'Robots' and 'Blocks'").
3. Roblox launched to the public on September 1, 2006.
   Source: Wikipedia infobox (same URL as 1), Release: September 1, 2006.
4a. As of February 2025, Roblox reported an average of 85.3 million daily
   active users.
   Source: Wikipedia (same URL as 1).
4b. Back in 2020, Roblox said over half of all American children under
   the age of 16 played Roblox. The claim originates in April 2020
   (The Verge, from Roblox: "played by over half of all children in the
   United States under the age of 16", 120M monthly players at the time)
   and was repeated in Roblox's S-1 filing of November 2020 ("played by
   over half of all children aged under 16 in the US", 164M monthly
   players as of August 2020). Wikipedia still reports it as the
   company's claim alongside the Feb 2025 DAU figure, but the evidence
   is from 2020 — so the video dates it to 2020 and never presents it
   as a current measurement.
   Sources: https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-played-by-most-american-kids/
   ; https://www.edexlive.com/news/2020/nov/20/child-friendly-gaming-firm-roblox-files-to-go-public-after-increased-use-during-pandemic-16003.html
   ; Wikipedia (same URL as 1).
5. The Developer Exchange (DevEx) lets creators turn Robux into real money;
   it launched in 2013 (Robux itself arrived in 2007).
   Source: https://inleo.io/threads/view/askrafiki/re-taskmaster4450le-1763821690
   ("Roblox introduced Robux… in 2007… DevEx (Developer Exchange) in 2013
   to let creators cash out earnings.")

## Deliberately NOT claimed

- No monthly-active-user figure (sources disagree; only the dated 85.3M DAU
  figure is used, labelled "today" with the Feb 2025 date in this brief).
- No "millionaire creator" claim (unverified in the sources consulted).
- No current game rankings (those belong to the Top 5 video).
