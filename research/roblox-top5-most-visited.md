# Research brief — roblox-top5-most-visited

Subject: the five most-visited Roblox games of all time, ranked by lifetime
visits. Format: ROBLOX TOP 5. Target 30–45 s.

Lifetime visits, snapshot November 2026. Three independent trackers agree
within ~0.5% on all five ranks:

Sources:
- https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_places_with_the_most_visits
  (list last updated November 18, 2026)
- https://rblxdb.com/records (Roblox lifetime counters)
- https://www.demandsage.com/how-many-people-play-roblox/
  (Brookhaven 89.6B, Steal a Brainrot 74.5B, Blox Fruits 65.5B,
  Adopt Me! 45.4B, Grow a Garden 36B)

Derived figure used in the script: 89.7 + 74.5 + 65.6 + 45.5 + 36.0 =
311.3 billion, stated as "over 311 billion visits" (311.3 > 311).
Correction v2 (22 Nov 2026, Amine's review): the v1 payoff showed
"310 BILLION VISITS." while the five displayed pills sum to 311.3B —
an arithmetic inconsistency. The v2 script and payoff title now say
"over 311 billion visits" / "OVER 311 BILLION VISITS.".
Correction v3 (22 Nov 2026, Amine's review): the voice rounded
differently from the badges ("45 / 65 / 74 billion" for 45.5 / 65.6 /
74.5), so a viewer adding the spoken numbers got 310, not 311. The v3
script says "about 45 and a half / about 65 and a half / about 74 and a
half billion" — spoken sum 36 + 45.5 + 65.5 + 74.5 + 90 = 311.5,
consistent with "over 311 billion" and with the badges.

## Labelling

- The script says "counted in November 2026" — visit counts keep rising,
  so the ranking is a dated snapshot, not a permanent fact.
- "Lifetime visits" (times players entered a game), NOT current players —
  the script never calls these "most played right now".
