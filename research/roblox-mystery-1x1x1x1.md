# Research brief — roblox-mystery-1x1x1x1

Subject: the 1x1x1x1 legend — how a test account became Roblox's biggest
myth, and what the truth was. Format: ROBLOX MYSTERIES. Target 30–45 s.

## Verified claims (fact vs rumor kept separate)

FACT — the account was created by a Roblox admin for lore:
- 1x1x1x1 was created by Shedletsky (formerly Telamon) on October 25,
  2007, as a test/lore account, after the name first appeared in the
  original description of the Necrobloxicon item.
  Source: https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:1x1x1x1

RUMOR — players believed it was a hacker:
- Rumors spread that 1x1x1x1 was an exploiter / leader of an exploiting
  group who could hack accounts and destroy games.
  Source: same Fandom page ("many rumors spread about 1x1x1x1 being an
  exploiter or a leader of an exploiting group").

FACT — exploiters borrowed the name:
- In December 2007 an exploit script related to 1x1x1x1 appeared; in
  2008–2009 exploiters wrecked popular games while disguising themselves
  as 1x1x1x1.
  Source: same Fandom page.

FACT — the debunk:
- In 2011, former Roblox admin MrDoomBringer debunked the claims in a
  forum post: the original account was Shedletsky's test account, and the
  in-game chaos came from various exploiters using the lore to scare
  people.
  Source: https://craveu.ai/tags/1x1x1x1-forsaken ("In 2011, former
  Roblox admin MrDoomBringer stepped forward to set the record straight…
  the original 1x1x1x1 account was indeed a test account created by
  Shedletsky.")

FACT — the legend lives on:
- 1x1x1x1 was reintroduced as a killer in the horror-survival game
  Forsaken, and Roblox has referenced him in media and events.
  Source: https://craveu.ai/tags/1x1x1x1-forsaken ; Fandom page.

## Verification (v3, 22 Nov 2026, at Amine's request)

Both factual claims Amine flagged were re-checked against the sources
above plus two more, on 22 Nov 2026 — both hold exactly as scripted:

1. "Necrobloxicon, 2007" — VERIFIED. The Necrobloxicon hat was released
   in the Roblox catalog on October 24, 2007; its original description
   first mentioned the name 1x1x1x1 ("...a being of pure hatred with the
   name of One-by-One-by-One-by-One..."). The 1x1x1x1 account itself was
   created by Shedletsky the next day, October 25, 2007.
   Sources: https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:1x1x1x1 ("created by
   Shedletsky, formerly Telamon on October 25th, 2007… following the
   first mention of 1x1x1x1 in the original description of The
   Necrobloxicon"); https://mythcommunity.miraheze.org/wiki/1x1x1x1
   (hat uploaded Oct 24, 2007, with the quoted description; account
   created the next day).
2. "Test account created by admin Shedletsky, revealed 2011" — VERIFIED.
   Former Roblox admin MrDoomBringer's forum post of July 13, 2011
   confirmed 1x1x1x1 was never a hacker and originated as a fictional
   element from one of Shedletsky's creative backstories; the original
   account was indeed a test account created by Shedletsky.
   Sources: https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/1x1x1x1/Synopsis ("according
   to a forum post made by the former Roblox administrator MrDoomBringer
   on July 13, 2011, 1x1x1x1 was never a hacker or exploiter… originated
   as a fictional element from one of Shedletsky's creative backstories");
   https://craveu.ai/tags/1x1x1x1-forsaken ("In 2011, former Roblox admin
   MrDoomBringer stepped forward… the original 1x1x1x1 account was indeed
   a test account created by Shedletsky").

## Title (v2, 22 Nov 2026, Amine's review)

Video title: **"1x1x1x1: The Roblox Hacker Myth"**. The v1 hook title
"The hacker who never existed" could imply the account never existed —
it is the fictional hacker that never existed. The narration sentence
"The hacker never existed." is unchanged: in context ("He was never a
hacker. The hacker never existed.") it refers to the hacker persona,
not the account, whose existence as Shedletsky's test account is stated
in the same scene.

## Deliberately NOT claimed

- No claim that 1x1x1x1 himself hacked anyone (the script frames the
  hacking as what "players believed").
- No exact dates for the Forsaken appearance (undated in sources).
- The 2010 compromise / name-snipe of the account is omitted — too
  detailed for 40 seconds and not needed for the story.

> destroy your game, and ban you. His name was *1x1x1x1*. The name first
> appeared in 2007, in the description of a spooky Roblox hat called the

> never existed. But the legend did, and today he is back, as a killer in
> the game Forsaken.
