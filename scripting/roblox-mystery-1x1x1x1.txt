For years, players believed one username could hack your account,
destroy your game, and ban you. His name was *1x1x1x1*. The name first
appeared in 2007, in the description of a spooky Roblox hat called the
Necrobloxicon. Then exploiters wrecked games and signed their chaos
with his name, so every crash looked like proof. But in 2011, a Roblox
admin revealed the truth. 1x1x1x1 was a test account, created by an
admin named Shedletsky, for a story. He was *never* a hacker. The hacker
never existed. But the legend did, and today he is back, as a killer in
the game Forsaken.
