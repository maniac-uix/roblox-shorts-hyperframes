This game hit over 400 million visits in two months, and it is still top five on Roblox right now. It is called Throw Food For Animals, and it launched on September 18, 2026. You throw food as far as you can, animals come to eat it, and they join your collection. Your animals earn money while you play, and while you are away, so you can buy better food for rarer animals. Train on a pull-up bar to gain Throw Power, and unlock seventeen areas, from the Forest to the Nuclear Wasteland. Two months old, top five on Roblox. From noob to pro, try it this week.
