Starting December 2, try a game pass for free. Roblox adds a Try button to the Shop, right next to Buy. Tap it, and you unlock the whole pass, for one full session. One session means at least one hour of free play. One free trial per pass, and only one at a time. But the creator has to turn trials on first. A pass you already own shows no Try button at all. Buy it before the session ends, and you keep it for good. From noob to pro, try before you buy, starting December 2.
