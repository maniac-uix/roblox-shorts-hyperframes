Did you know Roblox is 22 years old? It started in 2004, under the name DynaBlocks. Roblox itself means robots plus blocks. It launched to the public on September 1, 2006. In February 2025, over *85 million* players log in daily. Back in 2020, this is what Roblox said. *Over half* of American kids under sixteen played it. And since 2013, creators can turn their Robux into *real money*. So the players build the games, and they get paid for it. So Roblox is not just a game. 22 years later, it is still built by its own players.
