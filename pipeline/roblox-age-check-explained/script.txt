Roblox now makes you prove your age before you can chat. Since November 2025, chat needs an age check for everyone. You film a short video selfie, or show an ID if you are thirteen or older. Persona estimates your age from it, then deletes the video right away. Roblox sorts you into one of six age groups, and you can only chat with your group and the ones next to it. A twelve-year-old can reach up to fifteen, but no one sixteen or older can message them. Chat stays off under nine unless a parent agrees. So your chat now depends on your age.
