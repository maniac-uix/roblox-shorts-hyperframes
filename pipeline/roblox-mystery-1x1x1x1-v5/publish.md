## Description (YouTube Shorts)

Could one username hack your account, destroy your game, and ban you? His name was 1x1x1x1. It first appeared in 2007, in the description of a spooky Roblox hat called the Necrobloxicon. Then exploiters wrecked games and signed their chaos with his name, so every crash looked like proof. But in 2011, a Roblox admin revealed the truth. It was a test account, created by an admin named Shedletsky, for a story. He was never a hacker. The hacker never existed. But the legend lives on — as a killer in the game Forsaken.
